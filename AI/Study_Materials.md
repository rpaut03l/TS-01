## 2. MIT & Stanford Lectures (YouTube)

### 🎓 MIT — Artificial Intelligence (Prof. Patrick Winston)

🔗 **[YouTube Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUl4u3cNGP63gFHB6xb-kVBiQHYe_4hSi)**

One of the most legendary AI courses ever taught. Prof. Winston's explanations are beautifully clear. Covers search, logic, learning, neural nets, and the "big ideas" in AI.

> 💡 **Recommended**: Watch the lectures on Search and Constraint Satisfaction — Winston explains them with incredible intuition.

### 🎓 Stanford — Artificial Intelligence (CS221)

🔗 **[YouTube Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rO1NB9TD4iUZ3qghGEGtqNX)**

Stanford's flagship AI course. Very modern, covers search, MDPs, game playing, Bayesian networks, and machine learning.

> 💡 **Recommended**: The MDP and Reinforcement Learning lectures are the clearest available anywhere.

### 🎓 UT Austin — CS343 Artificial Intelligence

🔗 **[Course Website](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~mooney/cs343/)**

Lecture notes, assignments, and resources. Good for additional practice problems.

### 🎓 MIT OCW — Society of Mind (Prof. Marvin Minsky)

🔗 **[Video Lectures](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-868j-the-society-of-mind-fall-2011/video_galleries/video-lectures/)**

A philosophical exploration of how intelligence works — by one of the founding fathers of AI. Not exam-focused, but fascinating for understanding the "why" behind AI.

## 3. Reference Textbooks

### 📕 Primary Text: Artificial Intelligence — A Modern Approach (AIMA)

**Authors**: Stuart Russell & Peter Norvig
**Edition**: 4th Edition (also 3rd Edition is fine)

This is THE standard AI textbook used worldwide. Syllabus maps directly to its chapters:

| Syllabus Topic | AIMA Chapters |
|---|---|
| Uninformed Search | Chapter 3 |
| Informed Search (A*) | Chapter 3.5-3.6 |
| Local Search | Chapter 4.1-4.2 |
| And-Or Search | Chapter 4.3-4.4 |
| Adversarial Search (Minimax, α-β) | Chapter 5 |
| CSP | Chapter 6 |
| Propositional Logic | Chapter 7 |
| First-Order Logic | Chapter 8 |
| FOL Inference | Chapter 9 |
| Planning (STRIPS, POP) | Chapter 10-11 |
| Bayesian Networks | Chapter 13-14 |
| Reinforcement Learning | Chapter 17, 22 |

### 📗 Online Reference Text

🔗 **[Artificial Intelligence: Foundations of Computational Agents (3rd Edition)](https://artint.info/3e/html/ArtInt3e.html)**

Free online textbook! Great for alternative explanations if AIMA is unclear on a topic.

## 4. Research Papers

### 📄 Can Machines Think? — Alan Turing (1950)

🔗 **[PDF](https://courses.cs.umbc.edu/471/papers/turing.pdf)**

THE founding paper of AI. Turing asks "Can machines think?" and proposes the famous Turing Test. A must-read for understanding where AI started.

> 💡 **Read this**: It's surprisingly accessible and beautifully written. Only ~30 pages.

### 📄 AI Search Algorithms — Survey Paper

🔗 **[PDF (ACM Digital Library)](https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.5555/1882723.1882745)**

A comprehensive survey of search algorithms in AI. Covers BFS, DFS, A*, IDA*, and more with analysis.
